<h1 align="center">⛽ Fuel Price Effect on Consumer Price Index in Malaysia</h1>

### NOTE: This README will summarize the whole pipeline. If you only want to see the final report visualization, jump to this Power BI [dashboard](#dashboard) I created.

## Business Task
To analyze historical fuel price trends and their relationship with inflation in Malaysia, providing insights into cost fluctuations and their potential impact on consumers and the broader economy.

## Dataset Used
|Dataset Title|Source|Description|
|---|---|---|
|<a href = 'https://open.dosm.gov.my/data-catalogue/cpi_lowincome'>Monthly CPI for Low-Income Households</a>|Open DOSM Data Catalogue|National-level CPI for low-income households (< RM3000 per month) across 13 main groups of goods and services.|
|<a href = 'https://open.dosm.gov.my/data-catalogue/fuelprice'>Price of Petroleum & Diesels</a>|Open DOSM Data Catalogue|Weekly retail prices of RON95 petrol, RON97 petrol, and diesel in Malaysia.|
